Got questions?

Frequently asked questions

Can't find the answer here? Contact our support team →

Is Academix really free to start?
Yes — the Free plan never expires and doesn't require a credit card. You get access to one subject and up to 10 lessons per month. Upgrade any time from your account dashboard if you want full access.
What age group is Academix designed for?
Academix is built for students in grades 5 through 12 (ages 10–18). Each subject has courses organised by grade level, so learners always start at the right point in their journey.
Are the certificates recognised by schools and colleges?
Academix certificates are widely accepted as supplementary academic credentials. While they are not accredited qualifications, many schools and colleges count them toward extracurricular achievements and college applications. Always check with your institution for specifics.
Can I switch or cancel my plan at any time?
Absolutely. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel your subscription at any time from your account settings. If you cancel, you'll keep access until the end of your current billing period — no hidden fees.
How do the live Q&A sessions work?
Pro and School plan members get access to weekly live sessions for each subject. Sessions are hosted by subject instructors and run for 45–60 minutes. You can submit questions in advance or ask live. All sessions are recorded and available for 30 days after broadcast.
Does the School plan include admin tools?
Yes. The School plan comes with a dedicated admin dashboard where you can add and manage up to 30 student seats, view individual progress reports, assign courses, and contact priority support. Perfect for tutoring centres, homeschool co-ops, and small schools.
